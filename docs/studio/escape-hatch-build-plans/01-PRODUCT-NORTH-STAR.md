# Product north star

## Product job

Give a Patreon creator a clear, trustworthy route from “my livelihood and archive live on someone else's platform” to “I own a working membership website and can operate it.”

The creator should not need to become a systems architect. Relay supplies the map, verifies the route, assembles the chassis, and hands over the vessel. The creator supplies their lawful content, brand, provider accounts, domain, and business decisions.

## Finished-product promise

After completing the wizard, the creator has:

- a live membership website on a provider URL, with custom-domain setup available;
- a source-controlled Next.js TypeScript application they may move or modify;
- their normalized posts, bodies, tiers, access rules, and media;
- creator-owned infrastructure accounts and credentials;
- working public/member/tier paywalls with no premium bytes in public storage;
- a creator admin for ongoing publishing and account operations;
- tested Patreon continuity and independent billing where policy allows;
- a downloadable ownership and operations packet.

Relay promises portability and control. Relay does not promise that a host, processor, registrar, or government will never enforce policy, change terms, or become unavailable.

## Experience doctrine

1. **Show truth before style.** The creator confirms what Relay extracted, what is missing, and which tier unlocks each post before branding.
2. **One decision at a time.** Every step explains the consequence, recommends a default, and offers a safe way back.
3. **Never strand the creator in a provider dashboard.** Open the exact external page, explain what to do, then automatically validate the result in Relay.
4. **Do not celebrate until it works.** “Ready” means accounts, access, media, checkout, webhook, deployment, and recovery checks passed.
5. **Independence is visible.** Distinguish required creator-owned services from optional Relay services. Show recurring third-party and Relay charges before launch.
6. **No dark-pattern retention.** Relay-managed Patreon verification must sit beside the creator-owned path, disclose cancellation effects, and include migration instructions.
7. **Failures are routes, not dead ends.** Preserve progress, state exactly what failed, and offer retry, change-provider, download-diagnostics, and human-escalation paths.

## Scope

### Included

- Post title, body, publication date, tags, embeds/links, images, video, audio, and downloadable attachments.
- Public, all-paid-members, exact-tier, and tier-or-higher access.
- Patreon OAuth continuity and independent account linking.
- Independent recurring subscriptions through a policy-compatible billing adapter.
- Native posts and optional read-only Patreon transition sync.
- Optional Relay Crosspost publishing through a revocable site API token.
- Creator admin: posts, scheduling, media, tiers, prices, access, patron status/overrides, branding, domain and service health.
- Provider-neutral Discord/community links.

### Excluded from v1

- Comments, favorites, social feed, DMs, forums, moderation queues, and community hosting.
- General drag-and-drop page building or arbitrary user-authored code in the wizard.
- Re-Populate email campaigns and audience solicitation automation.
- Relay custody of patron subscription proceeds.
- Percentage fees on creator subscription revenue.
- Claims that any provider is permanent or censorship-proof.
- Processing or deployment assistance for illegal, exploitative, non-consensual, or otherwise prohibited content.

## Ownership doctrine

The creator owns their:

- imported and normalized data;
- copied media and integrity manifests;
- generated site instance and its configuration;
- domain, provider accounts, customer records, and billing relationship;
- credentials and backups;
- modifications to their delivered package.

Relay retains its reusable generator and generic chassis IP. The delivered package includes a broad, perpetual right to run, modify, migrate, and self-host that copy without an active Relay subscription.

The ownership manifesto is product UI and a handoff artifact, not marketing fine print.

## Commercial boundary

- **Base product:** one-time Escape Hatch construction and handoff fee; amount remains a finance decision.
- **Managed Patreon verification:** optional monthly Relay add-on for OAuth mediation, token maintenance, entitlement assertions, and connector uptime.
- **Maintenance:** defects present at delivery are covered for 90 days. Feature work, dependency modernization, custom modifications, provider migrations, and hands-on operations are separately priced.
- **Future managed hosting:** permitted as a later opt-in upkeep service, but generated sites must not require it.

## Lawful controversial and adult creators

Relay is a neutral technical facilitator for lawful creators. It must:

- collect creator attestations and stop on prohibited content;
- use dated, official provider-policy evidence;
- route creators only to providers whose stated policies fit the declared use;
- never disguise content, transaction purpose, or merchant identity;
- keep hosting, storage, email, and billing adapters replaceable;
- provide migration/export tools so a policy change does not destroy the site.

Stripe's published policy prohibits pornography and mature content designed for sexual gratification. The wizard must not offer Stripe for an ineligible use. A non-Stripe adapter may ship only after legal/policy review, sandbox verification, webhook parity, and human approval.

## Success measures

- At least 98% post/tier/media parity on sampled creator exports.
- Zero unauthorized premium-media retrieval in automated and staging attack tests.
- At least 90% of usability-test creators reach a validated preview without staff intervention.
- At least 80% reach a live provider URL in one guided session when required accounts are available.
- Every completed handoff has a verified restore path and no undisclosed Relay runtime dependency.
- A creator can replace Relay-managed Patreon verification with creator-owned OAuth without rebuilding or losing linked site accounts.
